Withdrawal Policy

Information about the statutory right of withdrawal for consumers.

1. Right of Withdrawal

If you are a consumer, you generally have a statutory right of withdrawal in accordance with applicable law.

You have the right to withdraw from the purchase contract within 14 days without giving any reason.

2. Withdrawal Period

For contracts concerning goods, the withdrawal period generally begins when you, or a third party designated by you who is not the carrier, receive the goods.

The withdrawal period is 14 days.

3. How to Exercise Your Right of Withdrawal

To exercise your right of withdrawal, you must inform PM Chords of your decision to withdraw from the contract by means of a clear statement.

You may send your withdrawal request by email to:

pmchordshq@gmail.com

You may use the model withdrawal form below, but you are not required to do so.

It is sufficient to send your withdrawal communication before the withdrawal period has expired.

4. Consequences of Withdrawal

If you withdraw from the contract, we will reimburse all payments received from you, including the cost of standard delivery, without undue delay and in accordance with the applicable statutory requirements.

The reimbursement will generally be made using the same payment method that was used for the original transaction, unless another arrangement has been expressly agreed.

5. Return of Goods

You must return the goods without undue delay and, in any event, no later than 14 days from the day on which you informed us of your decision to withdraw from the contract.

The deadline is met if you send the goods before the period of 14 days has expired.

7. Condition of Returned Goods

You are responsible for any diminished value of the goods resulting from handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of the goods.

8. Exceptions to the Right of Withdrawal

The statutory right of withdrawal does not apply in certain cases defined by law.

This may include goods that are not prefabricated and are made according to an individual choice or decision by the consumer, or goods that are clearly personalised.

Whether an exception applies depends on the specific product and circumstances of the order.
