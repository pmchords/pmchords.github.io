Shipping & Returns

Information about shipping, delivery and returns for purchases from PM Chords.

1. Shipping

PM Chords aims to offer worldwide shipping. Shipping availability may depend on the destination.

If shipping to a particular destination is not possible, this will be communicated before the purchase is confirmed.

2. Production & Delivery

Each piece is made after a request has been submitted and the purchase has been confirmed by PM Chords.

The general process is: Request → Confirmation → Production → Shipping.

The expected production and delivery time will be communicated before the purchase is confirmed.

3. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs depend on the destination and the selected shipping method.

The applicable shipping costs will be communicated before the purchase is confirmed, so that the total price is known before the contract is concluded.

4. Returns

Consumers generally have a statutory right of withdrawal for distance contracts. Further information about the right of withdrawal and how to exercise it can be found in our Withdrawal Policy.

The statutory rights concerning defective or incorrectly delivered goods remain unaffected.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your item arrives damaged or you receive an incorrect item, please contact us as soon as possible at pmchordshq@gmail.com .

We will review the issue and work with you to find an appropriate solution in accordance with your statutory rights.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about shipping, delivery or returns, please contact:

We’re happy to help.
